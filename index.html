Our Forensic Services

Scientific examination across criminal, civil & corporate cases with court-admissible reporting

Questioned Document Examination

Scientific analysis of disputed documents using forensic instrumentation:

  • Handwriting & Signature: Authorship ID, forgery & disguised writing detection
  • Ink & Paper Analysis: Age determination, TLC, spectroscopy, paper dating via VSC
  • Alterations: Erasures, obliterations, additions detected using ESDA & UV/IR
  • Charred/Indented Writing: Recovery of burnt documents & invisible impressions

Fingerprint Examination

Advanced forensic fingerprint analysis for criminal & civil cases:

  • Latent Print Development: Powder, chemical & fuming methods on all surfaces
  • Comparison & Identification: AFIS, ridge detail analysis, minutiae matching
  • Digital Analysis: Enhanced prints from photos, documents, mobile screens
  • Expert Opinion: Court-ready reports u/s 45 Evidence Act with demonstrative charts

Crime Scene Investigation

Independent scientific reconstruction & evidence documentation:

  • Scene Processing: Photography, videography, sketching, evidence collection
  • Bloodstain Pattern Analysis: Impact, cast-off, transfer patterns for sequence reconstruction
  • Trace Evidence: Hair, fiber, glass, paint, soil collection & analysis
  • Reconstruction: Scientific sequencing of events based on physical evidence

Insurance Investigation

Scientific scrutiny of questionable claims through forensic methods:

  • Fire Claims: GC-MS for accelerant detection, electrical forensics
  • Motor Claims: Crush analysis, momentum vectors, staged accident verification
  • Document Fraud: ESDA & VSC for policy forgery & indented writing
  • Admissible Opinion: Reports u/s 45 Evidence Act with chain of custody
