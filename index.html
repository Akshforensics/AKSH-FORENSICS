Fingerprint Examination

Fingerprint examination is one of the most reliable methods of personal identification in forensic science. Friction ridge patterns are unique and permanent, making fingerprints invaluable for criminal investigations, civil disputes, employee verification, identity confirmation, and court proceedings.

Types of Fingerprints

Latent Prints

Invisible impressions left by sweat and natural skin secretions. These require scientific development techniques before comparison.

Patent Prints

Visible fingerprints produced by substances such as blood, ink, grease, dust or paint.

Plastic Prints

Three-dimensional impressions left on soft materials such as wax, clay, soap or putty.

Fingerprint Development Techniques

Powder Dusting
Cyanoacrylate (Super Glue) Fuming
Ninhydrin Treatment
Iodine Fuming
Silver Nitrate Processing
Fluorescent Dye Staining
Alternate Light Source (ALS)

Questioned Document Examination

Questioned Document Examination is a scientific process used to determine the authenticity of handwriting, signatures, printed documents, inks, papers, stamps, and alterations. Our examinations follow internationally accepted forensic methodologies and produce court-admissible expert reports.

Our Examination Services

Handwriting Examination

Comparison of natural handwriting characteristics including letter formation, spacing, slant, rhythm, pen pressure, movement and individual writing habits.

Signature Verification

Identification of genuine and forged signatures through microscopic examination and comparison of writing characteristics.

Forgery Detection

Detection of traced signatures, simulated writing, free-hand forgery, copied signatures and disguised handwriting.

Document Alteration

Identification of additions, overwriting, erasures, obliterations, substitutions and tampered entries.

Ink Examination

Comparative examination of inks to identify differences between writings and determine possible alterations.

Paper & Print Analysis

Scientific examination of paper, printing processes, photocopies, laser printers and inkjet printers.
