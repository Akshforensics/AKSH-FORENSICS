Fingerprint Examination

Understanding Fingerprint Examination

Fingerprint examination is one of the most reliable methods of personal identification in forensic science. The uniqueness and permanence of friction ridge patterns make fingerprints invaluable for linking individuals to crime scenes and verifying identity.

Types of Fingerprints
  • Latent Prints: Invisible impressions left by sweat and natural skin secretions, requiring forensic development techniques.
  • Patent Prints: Visible fingerprints created by substances such as blood, ink, paint, grease, or dust.
  • Plastic Prints: Three-dimensional impressions left on soft materials like wax, soap, clay, or putty.
Development Techniques
  • Powder Dusting
  • Cyanoacrylate (Super Glue) Fuming
  • Ninhydrin Treatment
  • Iodine Fuming
  • Silver Nitrate Processing
  • Fluorescent Dye Staining
  • Alternate Light Source (ALS) Examination
Examination Process
  1. Evidence Collection
  2. Development of Latent Prints
  3. Digital Enhancement
  4. Comparison with Known Prints
  5. Verification by Independent Examiner
  6. Expert Report Preparation
Applications

Criminal Investigations, Civil Disputes, Identity Verification, Employee Fraud Cases, Property Crime Investigation, Court Evidence
